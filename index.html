The Ambala Legacy

Why Brides Across the World Choose Kuljit Fashions Ambala

For over four decades, the historic Ambala Cloth Market (Kapda Market) in Haryana has stood as the proud textile epicentre of North India. Celebrated for supplying India's most prestigious fashion designers, Ambala is renowned for offering royal craftsmanship at genuine wholesale pricing.

At Kuljit Fashions Ambala, we carry forward this rich lineage with our team of generational master karigars (artisans). Every zardozi petal, every gotapatti fold, and every Banarasi kadhwa bootie is woven with meticulous passion, giving our brides unmatched heirloom garments that last for generations.

Everything you need to know about custom bridal tailoring, international shipping, and Ambala Cloth Market shopping.

Ambala Cloth Market in Haryana is one of the oldest and largest wholesale textile and bridal hubs in North India. For decades, it has served as the core manufacturing hub supplying designers, boutiques, and brides across Punjab, Delhi, and overseas. At Kuljit Fashions Ambala, you purchase directly from master artisans and weavers without middlemen markups, saving up to 40-50% compared to metro luxury boutiques.

Once confirmed, we stitch, inspect, and ship via DHL/FedEx Express.
