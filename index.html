Visa Requirements for Indian Passport Holders

✓ Visa-Free Entry

Indian citizens can enter Malaysia without requiring an advance visa for social visits and tourism stays of up to 30 days. You only need to register the digital arrival card.

Document Checklist at Immigration Counter:

  • Indian Passport valid for at least 6 months beyond intended date of departure
  • Confirmed return air ticket back to India or onward destination
  • Confirmed hotel bookings or Kriti Tours official voucher
  • Completed Malaysia Digital Arrival Card (MDAC) submitted within 3 days of arrival (Complimentary filing assistance provided by Kriti Tours)
  • Proof of sufficient maintenance funds (Credit card / Cash min. USD 500 equivalent)

When is the Best Time to Travel to Malaysia?

Malaysia has a tropical climate year-round with temperatures hovering around 26°C to 32°C. The ideal travel window depends on which coast you want to explore:

West Coast (KL, Langkawi, Penang)

December to April

Dry and sunny days. Perfect for beach strolls in Langkawi and sightseeing in Kuala Lumpur.

East Coast & Diving Islands

April to September

Calm waters and superb underwater visibility at Perhentian and Redang islands.

Borneo Rainforests (Sabah)

March to October

Best wildlife viewing season for orangutans, pygmy elephants, and climbing Mount Kinabalu.

Pure Veg, Jain & Indian Dining Comfort

Malaysia has one of the world's most vibrant Indian communities. Indian travelers will feel right at home with hundreds of authentic vegetarian and South/North Indian restaurants!

Pure Veg & Jain Friendly

Saravanaa Bhavan, Annalakshmi, and Woodlands operate multiple branches in KL and Penang. Jain meals without onion or garlic can be customized easily upon prior intimation.

Must-Try Local Delicacies

Fluffy Roti Canai served with piping hot dhal sambar, Cendol (shaved ice coconut dessert), fresh tropical mangosteens, and sweet tender coconuts.

Malaysian Ringgit (MYR) & Currency Advice

The official currency is the Malaysian Ringgit (MYR or RM). 1 MYR is roughly equivalent to ₹18 to ₹20 INR.

  • Indian Forex Cards (Niyo, ICICI, HDFC) work at zero mark-up in ATMs and shopping malls
  • Carry 200–300 MYR in cash for street food night markets like Jalan Alor and petty tipping
  • Kriti Tours offers verified doorstep Forex exchange delivery before departure
