# 🌴 KRITI TOURS AND TRAVELS - Malaysia Holiday Portal

A modern, high-converting luxury travel portal for **KRITI TOURS AND TRAVELS**, offering handcrafted Malaysia holiday and tour packages for Indian travelers.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Distinct Luxury Design & Palette**: Deep Royal Sapphire (`#071729`, `#0b1e36`) with Radiant Amber Gold (`#f59e0b`) and Tropical Lagoon Teal (`#0d9488`).
- **Live Multi-Currency Engine**: Seamless toggle between **₹ INR**, **$ USD**, and **RM MYR** across all package rates, EMI estimates, and dynamic calculator totals.
- **Curated Malaysia Tour Packages**:
  - *Gems of Malaysia: Kuala Lumpur & Genting Highlands (5D/4N)*
  - *Romantic Malaysian Paradise: Langkawi Island & Kuala Lumpur (6D/5N)*
  - *Grand Malaysia Trio: KL + Penang + Langkawi (7D/6N)*
  - *Theme Park Extravaganza: Sunway Lagoon & Legoland (6D/5N)*
  - *Wild Borneo Safari & Mount Kinabalu (7D/6N)*
  - *Express Malaysia City Break: KL & Putrajaya (4D/3N)*
- **Interactive Day-by-Day Timeline Modal**: Detailed daily plans, inclusions & exclusions checklists, and direct WhatsApp booking.
- **Dynamic Live Price Calculator**: Instant pricing estimator adjusting for adults, children, and hotel comfort tiers (3-star, 4-star, 5-star).
- **Interactive Custom Tour Builder**: Multi-step wizard to design bespoke Malaysian holidays.
- **Indian Traveler Guide**: Up-to-date guidance on **30-day Visa-Free rules**, **MDAC filing**, **best travel seasons**, and **Pure Veg / Jain dining**.
- **Responsive Layout**: Optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop screens with floating WhatsApp and hotline action buttons.
