# 👑 KULJIT FASHIONS AMBALA — Royal Bridal & Ethnic Haute Couture

An ultra-luxurious, high-converting digital storefront and bridal trousseau portal for **Kuljit Fashions Ambala** (domain: `kuljitfashionsambala.com`), celebrating the legendary 40+ year heritage of **Ambala Cloth Market (Dev Samaj College Road, Ambala City, Haryana)**.

Taking inspiration from the high-converting conversion architecture, structured utility bar, interactive quick-filter hero, and customer trust elements of commercial portals (like malaysiatours.com.my), this project features a **completely distinct, opulent Indian bridal and ethnic luxury theme, rich imagery, royal colour palette, and bespoke feature set**.

---

## 🌟 Brand & Theme Distinctives

- **Opulent Indian Haute Couture Aesthetics**: Deep Royal Burgundy (`#4A0E2E`, `#2A0518`), Regal Champagne Gold (`#C99738`, `#F4E4BA`), Silk Pearl Ivory (`#FCFAF7`), and Emerald Zari accents.
- **Imperial Typography**: Display headings in *Cinzel* and *Playfair Display* paired with crisp *Plus Jakarta Sans* for modern legibility.
- **Authentic Bridal Imagery**: High-resolution imagery representing authentic Indian bridal lehengas, pure Banarasi silk sarees, Punjabi Patiala salwar suits, and Indo-Western reception gowns.

---

## ✨ Key Conversion & Interactive Features

1. **Live Multi-Currency Engine**:
   - Real-time conversion across **₹ INR**, **$ USD**, **C$ CAD**, **£ GBP**, **A$ AUD**, and **AED د.إ** across all product cards, quick view modals, and interactive calculators.
2. **Interactive Couture Catalog**:
   - Instant filtering across **Bridal Lehengas**, **Punjabi Salwar Suits**, **Pure Silk Sarees**, **Indo-Western & Gowns**, and **Boutique Unstitched Fabrics**.
   - Dynamic live keyword search & multi-criteria sorting (Price Low-to-High, High-to-Low, Highest Rated).
3. **Hero Quick Collection Finder**:
   - Instant selector by Category, Occasion (Wedding, Sangeet, Reception), Price Tier, and Stitching preference.
4. **Product Quick View / Detail Modal**:
   - Multi-angle image switcher, detailed fabric & embroidery specifications, lead times, and tailoring preference selector (Made-to-Measure, Semi-Stitched, Unstitched).
   - Pre-filled **1-Click WhatsApp Order generator** with item name, SKU, price in chosen currency, and tailoring selection.
5. **NRI Virtual Video Shopping Suite**:
   - Dedicated booking engine designed for NRI brides in Canada, the UK, the USA, Australia, and the Middle East for 1-on-1 HD showroom video calls with master stylists.
6. **Interactive Bridal Trousseau Wardrobe Budget Planner**:
   - Allows brides and families to customize their wedding wardrobe checklist (Bridal Lehenga, Reception Gown, Sangeet Anarkali, Mehendi Sharara, Banarasi Saree, Family Sets) with dynamic live budget tallying and instant WhatsApp quote booking.
7. **Ambala Cloth Market Heritage & Karigar Story**:
   - Narrative highlighting Ambala's status as North India's textile capital, 40+ years legacy, and direct wholesale pricing saving 40-50% vs metro showrooms.
8. **Real Bride Testimonials**:
   - Verified feedback and reviews from brides in Canada (Brampton), the UK (Southall), Delhi, and Chandigarh.
9. **Showroom Visit & Navigation Guide**:
   - Physical store address (Shop #42-45, Dev Samaj College Road, Cloth Market, Ambala City), store hours (Open 7 Days), and Google Maps integration.
10. **Floating Action Suite**:
    - Pulsing direct WhatsApp concierge, Hotline call button, and smooth back-to-top button.
